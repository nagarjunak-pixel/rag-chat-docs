ACME Tech Startup — Business Report Q4 2025

Company Overview
ACME Tech is a SaaS company founded in 2023, headquartered in Hyderabad, India.
We build AI-powered automation tools for small and medium businesses.
Our mission is to make AI accessible to companies that can't afford dedicated AI teams.

Revenue Model
Our revenue model is based on monthly subscription fees. We offer three tiers:
- Starter: Rs 2,000/month for up to 5 users
- Pro: Rs 8,000/month for up to 25 users
- Enterprise: Rs 25,000/month for unlimited users
All plans include a 14-day free trial. No setup fees.

Q4 2025 Financial Results
Total revenue for Q4 2025 was Rs 45 lakhs, up 35% year-over-year.
This exceeded our target of Rs 40 lakhs by 12.5%.
The breakdown by tier:
- Starter tier: Rs 12 lakhs (267 subscribers)
- Pro tier: Rs 28 lakhs (350 subscribers)
- Enterprise tier: Rs 5 lakhs (20 subscribers)

Customer Metrics
We ended Q4 with 637 active subscribers, up from 410 in Q3.
Customer churn rate decreased to 4.2% (down from 6.1% in Q3).
Net Promoter Score improved to 52 (up from 41 in Q3).
Average revenue per user (ARPU) is Rs 706/month.

Product Updates in Q4
We launched three major features in Q4:
1. AI Document Analyzer — extracts data from PDFs and images
2. Workflow Builder — visual drag-and-drop automation builder
3. API Marketplace — third-party integrations with 50+ services

Marketing Spend
Total marketing spend in Q4 was Rs 8 lakhs, representing 17.8% of revenue.
Breakdown:
- Digital ads: Rs 4.5 lakhs (Google Ads, LinkedIn, Twitter)
- Content marketing: Rs 2 lakhs (blog, YouTube, case studies)
- Events: Rs 1.5 lakhs (startup conferences, trade shows)

Team and Hiring
We grew from 18 to 27 employees in Q4.
Key hires: 2 ML engineers, 1 product designer, 3 sales executives, 1 HR manager.
Current open positions: Senior Backend Engineer, DevOps Lead, Marketing Manager.

Q1 2026 Plans
We plan to launch our enterprise AI agent platform in February 2026.
Target revenue for Q1 2026: Rs 60 lakhs.
Planning to hire 8 more engineers and open a sales office in Bangalore.
Board approved a 20% increase in marketing budget for Q1 2026.

Challenges and Risks
1. Competition from larger SaaS companies entering the AI space
2. Customer acquisition cost has risen 15% in the last 6 months
3. Need to scale infrastructure to handle 10x growth projected for 2026
4. Talent retention risk — two competitors are hiring aggressively in Hyderabad
